Applicant Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This notice explains how AB Systems Engineering handles personal data submitted by professionals who express interest in employment, consulting, contract or project opportunities.

Who is responsible for your data?

AB Systems Engineering is the controller for the application information submitted through this website. Privacy questions or requests can be sent to info@absystemsengineering.com.

What we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, location, professional experience, areas of expertise, availability, LinkedIn or portfolio links, CV, motivation letter and other information you choose to provide in your application.

Why we use it

We use application data only to review your suitability for current or potential professional opportunities, communicate with you about your application, and take steps connected with a possible working or consulting relationship.

Application transmission

The website currently uses FormSubmit as a form-delivery service to transmit application submissions to AB Systems Engineering by email. This means application data is processed by that service when you submit the form. Avoid including sensitive personal information that is not relevant to your professional application.

How long we keep applications

If we decide not to proceed with an application, we aim to delete the application data within four weeks after the relevant selection process ends, unless another retention period is legally required.

If you separately opt in to the talent pool on the application form, we may retain your application for up to 12 months so we can contact you about future opportunities. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us.

Who has access?

Access is limited to people involved in evaluating or administering professional opportunities for AB Systems Engineering and service providers used to transmit or host the information where necessary.

Your rights

Subject to applicable data-protection law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction concerning your personal data, and you may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact info@absystemsengineering.com to make a request.

Please do not upload identity documents, BSN numbers, medical information or other special-category personal data at the application stage unless AB Systems Engineering has specifically requested it for a lawful reason.

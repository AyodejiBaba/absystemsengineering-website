Applicant Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This notice explains how AB Systems Engineering handles personal data submitted for employment, consulting, contract or project opportunities.

Controller

AB Systems Engineering is the controller for application information submitted through this website. Contact info@absystemsengineering.com for privacy questions or requests.

Data we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, location, professional experience, expertise, availability, LinkedIn profile, CV, motivation letter and other information you choose to provide.

Purpose

We use this information to assess suitability for professional opportunities, communicate with you, and take steps connected with a possible working or consulting relationship.

Application transmission

The website currently uses FormSubmit to transmit applications to AB Systems Engineering by email. Do not include sensitive personal information that is not relevant to your application.

Retention

If we do not proceed, we aim to delete application data within four weeks after the relevant selection process ends, unless a different period is legally required. If you opt in to our talent pool, we may retain your application for up to 12 months. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Access and rights

Access is limited to people involved in evaluating or administering opportunities and necessary service providers. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, or withdraw consent where applicable.

Do not upload identity documents, BSN numbers, medical information or other special-category personal data unless specifically requested for a lawful reason.
